Coaching In person in Tallinn and online

Stronger, healthier, happier.

Hi! I'm Brit, a licensed personal trainer and your guide to a healthier lifestyle.

I've been strength training consistently for the past 10 years. Today it's my biggest passion and an inseparable part of my life, but my road here was neither straight nor easy. I've lived through anorexia, depression and anxiety myself. I know what constant self-criticism feels like, the need to control food, and how scary it is to start something new. I also know that hard, confusing moment when you want to change but don't know where to turn or who to look to.

The gym and mindful training helped me get to a better place with my body and my self-esteem.

Training isn't about punishing your body and desperately burning calories. It's about fuelling it, respecting it and getting stronger.

Today I want to be there for everyone struggling with the same things. My goal isn't to push you to extreme results through exhaustion and restriction, but to create a safe, supportive and judgment-free space where you can grow, learn to know your body and actually reach your goals.

My philosophyTraining doesn't take away, it gives back.

People often think a healthy lifestyle means strict rules, punishing diets and hours of exhausting training. My approach is different.

A healthy lifestyle doesn't have to mean sacrifices or going to extremes. Strength training that's built up properly and mindfully gives you:

  • More energy and joy in life Everyday tasks don't tire you out so quickly, and your sleep gets deeper.
  • Stronger mental and physical health Strength training relieves stress and anxiety, and strengthens your joints, bones and immune system.
  • Confidence and a functionally beautiful body You see and feel your body getting stronger, more resilient and more toned.
  • The freedom to enjoy life You don't have to give up dinners with friends or your favourite foods: balance is the foundation of everything.

Made for you if

  • You're a beginner and have never been to the gym
  • You already know your way around training, but want to train more mindfully
  • You want lasting change and a sustainable lifestyle that gives you a fit body and a happier mind

What you get

  • A personal training plan that actually helps you reach your goals
  • Feedback on your form from your videos
  • Online support, feedback and coaching
  • A trainer who's there for you every step of the way
